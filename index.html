    The Legacy of Zuari

    Zuari Infraworld India Limited (ZIIL) is the dedicated real estate arm of Zuari Industries Limited, part of the esteemed KK Birla Group, carrying forward a legacy that is deeply interwoven with India's growth story. Rooted in the visionary leadership of the late Dr. K. K. Birla, whose pioneering contributions helped shape key sectors of the nation, and guided today by eminent industrialist Mr. Saroj Kumar Poddar, Zuari Infraworld stands as a reflection of over eight decades of trust, excellence, and nation-building.

    As a key pillar of Zuari, ZIIL brings this rich heritage into the real estate space, creating thoughtfully designed residential and commercial developments that blend innovation, sustainability, and modern living. With a strong foundation built on integrity and a forward-looking vision, Zuari InfraWorld is committed to shaping contemporary urban landscapes while honoring the enduring values of its illustrious lineage.
