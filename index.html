    ABOUT KINETIX

    About Kinetix

    Where Life Plays Forward

    There are places you live in. And then there are places that move with you. Places that wake with the morning sun. That breathe with open skies. That echo with footsteps on green trails… with the laughter of children at play… with the thrill of sport in motion… and the quiet pride of building something of your own. Welcome to Zuari KinetiX — a landmark sports-themed plotted community in North West Bengaluru bang on the proposed 300Ft Intermediate Ring Road, created for those who believe life is meant to keep moving forward. This is more than land. More than infrastructure. More than an address. This is where movement becomes a lifestyle. Where wellness becomes everyday living. And where your future finds space to grow.

    Where Tomorrow Is Already Taking Shape

    Spread across ~15 acres along the rapidly emerging Nelamangala–Chikkaballapur growth corridor, Zuari KinetiX stands where North Bengaluru’s next chapter is unfolding. A location connected not just by roads—but by opportunity. With seamless access to the Satellite Town Ring Road, Peripheral Ring Road, airport corridor, railway connectivity, and key industrial zones, every direction leads toward growth. Surrounded by powerful economic drivers like Britannia Industries, KIADB developments, KWIN City, Foxconn’s expanding ecosystem, logistics parks, and fast-developing employment hubs—this is a location already in motion. And Zuari KinetiX places you right at the centre of it.

    Where Sport Becomes a Way of Life

    At Zuari KinetiX, sport is not just an amenity. It is identity. It is energy. It is the rhythm of the community. Designed around a bold sports-inspired masterplan, every avenue, every open space, and every landscape invites movement. Begin your mornings with a run through tree-lined boulevards. Challenge yourself on the pickleball court. Spend weekends at the football turf. Practice your swing, your sprint, your climb. Play basketball at sunset. Or simply watch your children discover the joy of open-air freedom. From cricket to football, pickleball to volleyball, archery to climbing walls—every space is crafted to inspire activity, connection and play. Here, life doesn’t slow down. It plays forward.

    Where Nature Lives Beside You

    Beyond movement comes balance. At Zuari KinetiX, nature is woven into everyday life—seamlessly, beautifully, intentionally. A vibrant green spine flows through the community, connecting wellness, recreation and peaceful open spaces. Walk through shaded gardens. Pause at the Five Sense Garden. Breathe deeper in the Miyawaki Forest. Stretch into sunrise at the Yoga Deck. Recharge in the open-air gym. Take an evening stroll through landscaped pathways as the day slows around you. Let your furry companions find their own little paradise at the dedicated Paw Park- thoughtfully designed with pet-friendly play equipment, open running space, and playful corners where every tail wags a little happier. Here, greenery is not a feature. It is a feeling. A place where skies feel wider. Air feels lighter. And every season feels closer to home.

    Build Your Home. Shape Your Future.

    At Zuari KinetiX, you don’t choose an apartment. You choose possibility. Choose from 251 premium residential plots, thoughtfully planned across sizes ranging from ~1,000 sq. ft. to ~2,000 sq. ft. Design a home that reflects your own pace, personality and vision. A private garden for quiet mornings. A courtyard filled with laughter. A terrace overlooking green open spaces. A home built around memories, movement and meaning. Because the most meaningful homes are never simply purchased. They are imagined. And then built—one dream at a time. They are imagined.     A Clubhouse That Celebrates Community

    At the heart of Zuari KinetiX rises a spectacular 20K+ sq. ft. clubhouse—crafted as the social heartbeat of the community. A place to gather. To celebrate. To recharge. To connect. Take a refreshing swim in the temperature-controlled pool. Train at the gym or fitness studio. Catch up over coffee at the café. Host celebrations in the banquet hall. Enjoy indoor games. Or gather with friends at the signature Sports Lounge to watch the big game together. Because some of life’s finest moments happen between plans.

    A Legacy You Can Build On

    Zuari KinetiX is brought to you by Zuari Infraworld, backed by the enduring trust of the Dr. KK Birla Group—a name built over generations through vision, credibility and excellence. A legacy trusted across India. A foundation built over decades. And now, a plotted community designed for the future of North Bengaluru. When you invest in Zuari KinetiX, you invest in more than a plot. You invest in trust. In lifestyle. In growth. In belonging. In a future that keeps moving forward.

    The Legacy of Zuari

    Zuari Infraworld India Limited (ZIIL) is the dedicated real estate arm of Zuari Industries Limited, part of the esteemed KK Birla Group, carrying forward a legacy that is deeply interwoven with India's growth story. Rooted in the visionary leadership of the late Dr. K. K. Birla, whose pioneering contributions helped shape key sectors of the nation, and guided today by eminent industrialist Mr. Saroj Kumar Poddar, Zuari Infraworld stands as a reflection of over eight decades of trust, excellence, and nation-building.

    As a key pillar of Zuari, ZIIL brings this rich heritage into the real estate space, creating thoughtfully designed residential and commercial developments that blend innovation, sustainability, and modern living. With a strong foundation built on integrity and a forward-looking vision, Zuari InfraWorld is committed to shaping contemporary urban landscapes while honoring the enduring values of its illustrious lineage.
